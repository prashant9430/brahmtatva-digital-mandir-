### जीवन-काल-कथन — 10 (19.1, 19.2, 19.4, 19.5, 19.6, 19.8, 19.9, 19.12, 19.14, 19.15)

- 19.1 — आयुर्भावफलं चाऽथ कथयामि द्विजोत्तम ।आयुःस्थानाधिपः केन्द्रे
  दीर्घमायुः प्रयच्छति — chapter address + 8L in kendra -> long life
  (favorable).
- 19.2 — आयुस्थानाधिपः पापैः सह तत्रैव संस्थितः ।करोत्यल्पायुषं जातं
  लग्नेशोऽप्यत्र संस्थितः — Sanskrit/vp: 8L with malefics in the 8th AND
  the lagna lord also placed there (stacked legs); jv splits the legs
  ("should the 8th lord join the ascendant lord OR a malefic and be in the
  8th") — AND-stack vs OR-split divergence recorded.
- 19.4 — षष्ठे व्ययेऽपि षष्ठेशो व्ययाधीशो रिपौ व्यये ।लग्नेऽष्टमे स्थितो
  वाऽपि दीर्घमायुः प्रयच्छति — 6L in the 12th or 6th; 12L in the 6th or
  12th (jv drops the 6th option for the 12L leg — divergence recorded);
  the third leg's subject is unstated in the Sanskrit — vp supplies
  "[the lord]", jv reads the 6th and 12th lords together in lagna/8th —
  the text is not settled; recorded.
- 19.5 — स्वस्थाने स्वांशके वाऽपि मित्रेशे मित्रमन्दिरे ।दीर्घायुषं
  करोत्येव लग्नेशोऽष्टमपः पुनः — L1 + 8L in own sign/own navamsha or
  friendly sign. Text-critical note: the capture reads मित्रेशे where
  मित्रांशे (friendly navamsha) is the expected parallel to स्वांशके —
  recorded. jv adds the 5th lord ("lords of the 5th, 8th and ascendant")
  — divergence recorded. Navamsha unmodeled (moot).
- 19.6 — लग्नाष्टमपकर्मेशमन्दाः केन्द्रत्रिकोणयोः ।लाभे वा
  संस्थितास्तद्वद् दिशेयुर्दीर्घमायुषम् — L1, 8L, 10L and Saturn
  severally in kendra/trikona/11th -> long life (favorable).
- 19.8 — अष्टमाधिपतौ केन्द्रे लग्नेशे बलवर्जते ।विंशद्वर्षाण्यसौ जीवेद्
  द्वात्रिंशत्परमायुषम् — 8L in kendra + weak L1 -> 20 years definite,
  32 as the maximum (Sanskrit/vp); jv reads a 20-32 range — divergence
  recorded. Lifespan numbers are a timed cross-note, not a count.
  Strength leg uncomputed (moot).
- 19.9 — रन्ध्रेशे नीचराशिस्थे रन्ध्रे पापग्रहैर्युते ।लग्नेशे दुर्बले
  जन्तुरल्पायुर्भवति ध्रुवम् — 8L debilitated + malefic in the 8th +
  weak L1 -> short life (adverse). Strength leg uncomputed (moot).
- 19.12 — पञ्चमे पापसंयुक्ते रन्ध्रेशे पापसंयुते ।रन्ध्रे
  पापग्रहैर्युक्ते स्वल्पमायुः प्रजायते — malefic with the 5th, with the
  8L, and in the 8th -> very brief life (adverse).
- 19.14 — लग्नेशे स्वोच्चराशिस्थे चन्द्रे लाभसमन्विते ।रन्ध्रस्थानगते
  जीवे दीर्घमायुर्न संशयः — L1 exalted + Moon joined with the 11th +
  Jupiter in the 8th -> long life (favorable). Sanskrit चन्द्रे
  लाभसमन्विते is ambiguous between the Moon with the 11th lord and the
  Moon in the 11th house; vp flags the ambiguity, jv reads the Moon in
  the 11th — recorded.
- 19.15 — लग्नेशोऽतिबली दृष्टः केन्द्रसंस्थैः शुभग्रहैः ।धनैः
  सर्वगुणैः सार्धं दीर्घमायुः प्रयच्छति — L1 exceedingly strong
  (अतिबली) + aspected by benefics placed in kendras -> wealth and all
  qualities TOGETHER WITH long life. The compound result cannot be
  silently split: the wealth/qualities leg is never served without the
  longevity leg, and the row takes its home here with a wealth/qualities
  cross-note, not a free wealth category. The exceedingly-strong leg is
  preserved in full; the strength framework is implementation work and
  the row stays unserved until it is properly computed.
