### 15.4 — विचित्र-सौध (mansions) [SERVE]

### 15.5 — बन्धु-पूज्य (honored by relatives) [SERVE as historical-tradition conditional]
Sanskrit: बन्धुस्थानेश्वरे सौम्ये शुभग्रहयुतेक्षिते ।शशिजे लग्नसंयुक्ते बन्धुपूज्यो भवेन्नरः

benefic — युतेक्षिते covers EITHER, not aspect-only) AND Mercury in lagna.

शर्त-कथन), NOT a person/reputation verdict about the native or relatives.
Scope is conservative for the initial partial preview; not a standing rule.

### 15.8 — गो-महिष्यादि लाभ (livestock gain) [SERVE]
Sanskrit: सुखे रवियुते मन्दे चन्द्रे भाग्यगते सति ।लाभस्थानगतो भौमो गोमहिष्यादिलाभकृत्
Condition: four AND legs — Sun in 4th; Saturn in 4th WITH Sun (सुखे
रवियुते मन्दे: Saturn in 4th, joined with Sun); Moon in 9th (चन्द्रे
भाग्यगते); Mars in 11th (लाभस्थानगतो भौमो).
jv reads "Moon and Saturn in the 9th" — divergence from Sanskrit recorded;
Sanskrit reading governs (Saturn in 4th with Sun, Moon alone in 9th).
