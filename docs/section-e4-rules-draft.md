## 2. संरचना (इस edition से verbatim-सत्यापित, OCR caveat सहित)
- **6.14 (Mahabhagya नियम)**: "If, at a day-birth in the case of a male, the Sun,
  the Moon and the Lagna are in odd signs, the mahabhagya Yoga is formed. The
  same Yoga in the case of females will arise when the birth is at night and the
  Sun, the Moon and the Lagna are posited in even signs."
  (उसी श्लोक में kesari/sakata/adhama-sama-varistha नियम भी — delivered।)
- **6.15 (Mahabhagya फल)**: "...will cause immense pleasure to all people, by his
  mere appearance, will be very liberal in his gifts and famous. He will be a
  ruler of the earth, **living for 80 years** and of a spotless character. If a
  female be born under this Yoga, she will be endowed with wealth, **a long-lived
  husband**, sons, and grandsons. She will be exceedingly lucky and well-behaved."
- **6.19 (Vasumat + Amala + Pushkala नियम)**: "If all the benefics occupy
  upachaya houses whether reckoned from the Lagna or the Moon, the resulting
  Yoga is termed vasumat. [Amala खंड — E3 में क्रियान्वित] If the lord of the
  Rasi occupied by the Moon is in conjunction with the lord of the Lagna and be
  posited in a Kendra or in the house of a very friendly plane[t] and if at the
  same time a benefic planet possessing strength aspect the Lagna, the resulting
  Yoga is called puskala."
- **6.20 (Vasumat/Amala/Pushkala फल)**: Vasumat = "will always keep to his house,
  and will command plenty of money." Amala फल दोहराव (E3 पंक्ति में है —
  खुला प्रश्न 9)। Pushkala = "wealthy, honoured by Kings, famous, decorated
  with beautiful ornaments and clothes, sweet-tongued, supremely good, a lord."
- **6.21 (Subhamala/Asubhamala + Lakshmi + Gauri नियम)**: "If all the benefic
  planets in regular order occupy the 5th, 6th and 7th houses, the Yoga is
  called subhamala. But if they should be posited in the 8th, 6th and 12th
  houses, it is called asubhamala. If the lord of the 9th and Venus be posited
  in their own or exaltation houses identical with a Trikona or a Kendra, the
  resulting Yoga is Lakshmi. If the Moon in the above position be aspected by
  Jupiter, the Yoga is Gauri."
  ⚠ पाठ-विशेषता: 6वाँ भाव दोनों सूचियों (5-6-7 और 8-6-12) में — overlap
  जानबूझकर दर्ज (खुला प्रश्न 4)।
- **6.22 (Subhamala फल)**: "governor (or director), extolled by Kings, devoted
  to enjoyment, liberal in gifts, helpful to others..., will love his relations,
  blessed with a good wife and sons, courageous."
- **6.23 (Asubhamalika फल)**: "will resort to bad ways, will be unhappy, **will
  tease or kill others**, will be ungrateful, will be timid, will have no
  reverence for Brahmins, will be cursed by the public and will be fond of
  (promoting) quarrels."
- **6.24 (Lakshmi फल)**: "ever be sporting with a damsel of a very amiable
  disposition. He will be **free from disease**, wealthy and brilliant... protect
  all his people, recipient of blessings from the Goddess of wealth, [palanquin/
  horse/elephant], best of Kings pleasing all his subjects, liberal in gifts."
- **6.25 (Gauri फल)**: "beautiful body, illustrious family, friend of the
  sovereign, sons of very good character, lotus-like face, success praised by
  one and all."

## 4. Detection नियम (प्रस्तावित)
- **Mahabhagya**: तीन शर्तें — (i) दिवा/रात्रि-जन्म (bala-इकाई का
  sunrise/sunset machinery; ध्रुवीय अपवाद-पथ मौजूद), (ii) लिंग, (iii) सूर्य/
  चन्द्र/लग्न सब odd (पुरुष-दिवा) या सब even (स्त्री-रात्रि) राशि में।
  ⚠ **लिंग-इनपुट फॉर्म में नहीं** → detection अपूर्ण (खुला प्रश्न 1)।
- **Vasumat**: सभी शुभ-वर्ग ग्रह (E1 classes — चन्द्र/बुध गतिशील) upachaya
  (3/6/10/11) में, लग्न से OR चन्द्र से। ⚠ upachaya सूची का श्लोक-पिन इस
  edition के ch6 में नहीं — पारंपरिक मानक 3/6/10/11; पिन खुला प्रश्न 5 के
  साथ। कोई शुभ बाहर → नहीं; अनिर्णीत-वर्ग सदस्य → अनिर्णीत (स्थापित नियम)।
- **Pushkala**: (a) चन्द्र-राशि-स्वामी + लग्न-स्वामी युति (same whole-sign);
  (b) वह युति kendra में OR अधिमित्र-ग्रह की राशि में (sambandha — relations
  module, खुला प्रश्न 3); (c) "balavan" शुभ की लग्न पर दृष्टि — **बल-सीमा
  अपरिभाषित** (खुला प्रश्न 2)।
- **Subhamala/Asubhamala**: सभी शुभ-वर्ग ग्रह {5,6,7} में → subhamala; सभी
  {6,8,12} में → asubhamala ("sarve" = सभी; set-membership; खुला प्रश्न 4)।
- **Lakshmi**: 9वें का स्वामी AND शुक्र — दोनों अपनी स्व/उच्च राशि में जो
  लग्न से kendra/trikona हो (स्व/उच्च गरिमा dashaContext में मौजूद)।
- **Gauri**: चन्द्र "उपर्युक्त स्थिति" में (स्व/उच्च + kendra/trikona — Sastri
  पठन) + गुरु की दृष्टि (5/7/9, E1 drishti module)।

## 5. Fixture-अपेक्षाएँ (प्रस्तावित)
1. Mahabhagya: odd/all-odd + दिवा (पुरुष) → बनी; even/all-even + रात्रि (स्त्री)
  → बनी; mixed-sign → नहीं; दिवा/रात्रि boundary (सूर्योदय ±) नियंत्रण;
  लिंग-अनुपलब्ध व्यवहार (Q1 निर्णय-निर्भर)।
2. Vasumat: सभी शुभ upachaya → बनी (लग्न-पक्ष / चन्द्र-पक्ष अलग asserts);
  एक शुभ बाहर → नहीं; अनिर्णीत-वर्ग सदस्य → अनिर्णीत।
3. Pushkala: प्रत्येक उप-शर्त अलग negative control (युति नहीं / kendra-
  अधिमित्र दोनों नहीं / शुभ-दृष्टि नहीं) → नहीं/अनिर्णीत (Q2-निर्भर)।
4. Subhamala exact-set → बनी; asubhamala exact-set → बनी; 6वाँ साझा होकर
  शेष विभाजित (जैसे शुभ {5,6,12}) → दोनों नहीं; अनिर्णीत-वर्ग → अनिर्णीत।
5. Lakshmi: दोनों dignity + kendra/trikona → बनी; एक नीच → नहीं।
6. Gauri: गुरु 5/7/9 दृष्टि → बनी; दृष्टि-रहित → नहीं; पाप-दृष्टि असंबंधित
   (फल-अवरोध नियम इस समूह में नहीं — स्पष्ट नोट)।
7. लोप invariants: "80", "अस्सी", दीर्घायु-पति, रोग-मुक्त, वध/himsa-पद absent;
   UI generic notice; निश्चय-शब्द scan; स्रोत-टैग + OCR caveat प्रति-पंक्ति।
