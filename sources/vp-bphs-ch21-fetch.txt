# Tenth House Career

अथ कर्मभावफलाध्यायः

Chapter 21 of Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra sets out 10th house career rules through the condition, position, and associations of the tenth lord and other planets. A strong tenth lord in exaltation, its own sign, or its own amsha is linked with wealth, fame, and good works.

The tenth lord with a benefic in a benefic house is linked with gains from the royal court and trade, while malefic combinations in the tenth and eleventh houses are linked with wrong acts and criticism of relatives. Jupiter's association with a strong tenth lord supports honor, resources, and comfortable living. The verses also give reputation rules, including the Moon in the tenth with its lord in a trine, and the eleventh lord in the tenth with a strong tenth lord aspected by Jupiter. Difficult combinations include a debilitated tenth lord with malefic influences.

The English below is our translation from the Sanskrit.

## This chapter in 4 points

1. 1 A strong tenth lord in exaltation, its own sign, or its own amsha is linked with wealth, fame, and good works. v.2
2. 2 The tenth lord joined with a benefic and placed in a benefic house is linked with gains from the royal court and trade. v.4
3. 3 The Moon in the tenth with its lord in a trine and the ascendant lord in a kendra is linked with good reputation. v.19
4. 4 A debilitated tenth lord with malefic planets in the tenth and related career positions is linked with difficulty in work.

## Common questions

What does this chapter examine?

It examines the results associated with the tenth house, especially the strength, sign, house position, and planetary associations of its lord. It also gives rules involving the Moon, Jupiter, Venus, Shani, Rahu, and other planets.

Which combinations support reputation and career?

The verses link good reputation with the Moon in the tenth when its lord is in a trine, and with the eleventh lord in the tenth when the tenth lord is strong and aspected by Jupiter. A strong tenth lord in a kendra or trikona with Jupiter is linked with engagement in work.

Which combinations indicate difficulty in work?

A weak tenth lord is linked with difficulty in work. The chapter also gives difficult rules involving a debilitated tenth lord and malefic planets in the tenth or related career positions.
