# Exact Planetary Aspects

अथे ग्रहस्फुटदृष्टिकथनाध्ययाः

Chapter 26 of Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra sets out exact planetary aspects, moving from the familiar sign-based rules to degree-based calculations. Verses 1-3 introduce the question and distinguish aspect by rashi from aspect arising from a planet's own nature.

Verse 4 states that every planet fully aspects the seventh house or sign; Shani also aspects the third and tenth, Jupiter the fifth and ninth, and Mars the fourth and eighth. Verse 3 adds that aspects connected with the third, fourth, seventh, and tenth positions, and with trinal positions, give results by increasing quarters. Verses 5-9 describe finding an exact aspect by comparing the seen point and the aspecting planet, using rashi, direction, degrees, and division by two. Verses 10-12 give further degree and portion rules for Shani, Mars, and Jupiter. Verse 13 says these exact aspects are used when stating results for the houses of a birth chart.

The English below is our translation from the Sanskrit.

## This chapter in 4 points

1 Every planet fully aspects the seventh house or sign from its position.

v.4

2 Shani additionally aspects the third and tenth, Jupiter the fifth and ninth, and Mars the fourth and eighth. v.4

3 The chapter distinguishes sign-based aspects from aspects calculated from the planets' exact positions. v.2

4 Verses 10-12 give additional degree and portion rules for Shani, Mars, and Jupiter.

त्रिदशे च त्रिकोणे च चतुरस्रे च सप्तमे ।पादवृद्धया प्रपश्यन्ति प्रयच्छन्ति फलं तथा

Planets in the 3rd [and 10th], in the trine, in the 4th [and 8th], and in the 7th aspect with increase by a quarter [each step], and give results accordingly (continued in the next verse).

7. ### Verse 26.6

दृश्याद् विशोध्य द्रष्टारं षड्राशिभ्योऽधिकान्तरम् ।दिगभ्यः संशोध्य तद्भागा द्विभक्ता दृक् स्फुटा भवेत्

Subtract the aspecting planet from the aspected one; if the difference exceeds six signs, subtract it from ten (dik) [signs, i.e. 300 degrees]; those degrees divided by two give the exact aspect (drik).

Simple meaning: Exact aspect: for a gap over 180 degrees, aspect = (300 minus the gap) / 2.

8. ### Verse 26.7

पञ्चाधिके विना राशिं भागाद्विघ्नाश्च दृक् स्फुटा ।वेदाधिके त्यजेद् भूताद् भागा दृष्टिः त्रिभाधिके

When [the difference] exceeds five [signs], leaving out the signs, the degrees multiplied by two (dvighna) are the exact aspect; when it exceeds four (veda) [signs], subtract [it] from five (bhuta) [signs]: the degrees are the aspect. When it exceeds three signs (continued in the next verse).

Simple meaning: Exact aspect: 150-180 degrees, twice the extra degrees; 120-150 degrees, 150 minus the gap.

9. ### Verse 26.8

विशोध्यार्णवतो द्वाभ्यां लब्धं त्रिंशद्युतं च दृक् ।द्व्यधिके तु विना राशिं भागास्तिथियुतास्तथा

Subtract from four (arnava); [halve it by two]; the quotient plus thirty is the aspect. When [the difference] exceeds two [signs], leaving out the sign, the degrees plus fifteen (tithi) [are the aspect].

Simple meaning: Aspect calculation: for 3-4 signs add 30 to half; for 2-3 signs add 15 to the degrees.

10. ### Verse 26.9

Saturn

रूपाधिके विना राशिं भागा द्वयाप्ताश्च दृग् भवेत् ।एवं राश्यादिके शेषे शनौ द्रष्टरि भो द्विज

When [the difference] exceeds one [sign], leaving out the sign, the degrees halved are the aspect. Thus, O twice-born, when signs etc. remain, where Saturn is the aspecting planet (continued in the next verse)

Simple meaning: Aspect calculation: for 1-2 signs, halve the degrees; Saturn's special rule follows.

11. ### Verse 26.10

एकभे नवभे भागा भुक्ता भोग्या द्विसंगुणाः ।द्विभेंऽशार्धोनिताः षष्टिरष्टभे खाग्नियुग् लवाः

[for Saturn], in the 1st and 9th [sign of difference], the degrees traversed and to be traversed are doubled; in the 2nd, sixty less half the degrees; in the 8th, the degrees plus thirty (continued from the previous verse).

Simple meaning: Saturn's aspect: 1st/9th sign, double the degrees; 2nd, 60 minus half; 8th, degrees plus 30.

12. ### Verse 26.11

Mars

त्रिसप्तभे तु भौमस्य षष्टिरत्र लवोनिता ।सार्धांशास्तिथिसंयुक्ता द्विभे रूपं सदाऽङ्गभे

For Mars, when [the difference] is three or seven signs [and some degrees], [the aspect] here is sixty less the degrees; when two signs, one and a half times the degrees (sardhamsha) plus fifteen (tithi); when six signs (anga), always one whole (rupa) [i.e. full aspect].

Simple meaning: Mars' exact aspect: stronger than usual at the 4th, 7th and 8th.

13. ### Verse 26.12

Jupiter

त्रिसप्तभे तु जीवस्य भागार्धं शरवेदयुक् ।द्विगुणैस्तु लवैश्चोनाः खरसाश्चतुरष्टभे

For Jupiter, in the 3rd and 7th, half the degrees plus forty-five (shara-veda); in the 4th and 8th, sixty (kha-rasa) less double the degrees.

Simple meaning: Jupiter's aspect: 3rd/7th, half the degrees plus 45; 4th/8th, 60 minus double.

## Common questions

Which aspects do all planets have?

Verse 4 says that every planet casts a full aspect to the seventh house or sign from where it is placed. Shani, Jupiter, and Mars also have additional aspects.

What additional aspects are given for Shani, Jupiter, and Mars?

Shani additionally aspects the third and tenth. Jupiter aspects the fifth and ninth, while Mars aspects the fourth and eighth.

How does the chapter calculate exact aspects?

Verses 5-9 describe a degree-based method using the positions of the aspecting planet and the point being viewed. Verses 10-12 add specific portion and degree rules for Shani, Mars, and Jupiter.
