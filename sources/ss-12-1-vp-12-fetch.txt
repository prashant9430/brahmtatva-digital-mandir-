# First House Results

अथ तनुभावफलाध्यायः

Chapter 12 of Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra sets out first house results through the lagna, its lord, the Moon, and planets influencing the ascendant. It says that a lagna lord in a kendra or trikona supports bodily comfort, while one joined to malefics or placed in the 6th, 8th, or 12th can reduce it.

Mercury, Jupiter, or Venus as lagna lord in a kendra or trikona is linked with longevity, wealth, intelligence, and royal favor. The verses also describe benefic and malefic effects on appearance and health concerns, including the role of aspects and conjunctions. They give rules for royal marks when Mercury, Jupiter, or Venus joins the Moon in the lagna or a kendra. The final verses map the three drekkanas to body parts and state that malefics indicate marks in the corresponding part.

The English below is our translation from the Sanskrit.

## This chapter in 4 points

1 A lagna lord in a kendra or trikona is said to support bodily comfort, while placement in the 6th, 8th, or 12th with malefic influence reduces it.

2 Mercury, Jupiter, or Venus as lagna lord in a kendra or trikona is linked with longevity, wealth, intelligence, and royal favor. v.5

3 Benefics in or aspecting the lagna support appearance and bodily comfort, while malefic influence without benefic aspect is unfavorable for them.

4 The first, middle, and third drekkanas map successively to body parts from the head through the trunk to the feet, with malefics indicating marks there.

## Common questions

What does this chapter judge from the first house?

It judges bodily comfort, appearance, health concerns, longevity, wealth, intelligence, and royal favor through the lagna, its lord, the Moon, and planetary influences. It also gives rules for royal marks and the indication of twins.

How does the chapter identify body marks?

The verses divide the body among the first, middle, and third drekkanas. A malefic in the relevant division is said to indicate a mark in the corresponding body part, while benefics indicate a mark.

Which planets support favorable first house results?

Mercury, Jupiter, and Venus receive especially favorable mention when connected with the lagna or its lord. Benefic planets in, joining, or aspecting the lagna are also said to support bodily comfort and appearance.
