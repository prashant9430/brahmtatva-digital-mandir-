युद्धम् अम्शुविमर्दाख्यम् अम्शुयोगे परस्परम् । अम्शाद् ऊने +अपसव्याख्यम् युद्धम् एको +अत्र चेद् अणुह्॥

When there is mutual mingling of rays, it is called angçuvimardwa (ray-obliteration). When the interval is less than a degree, the encounter (yuddha) is called apasavya (Dexter), and in this, if one star is smaller, then, it is called prakasha (visible) encounter and otherwise it is called aprakasha (invisible) encounter. If the interval is more than a degree and the planets are powerful (valavana), in this case, we have samägama.

रूक्षो विवर्णो विध्वस्तो विजितो दक्षिणाश्रितः। उदक्स्थो दीप्तिमान् स्थूलो जयी याम्ये +अपि यो बली॥

One that is rough, colorless, struck down (vidhvasta), situated to the south, is utterly vanquished (vijita). One situated to the north, having brilliancy, large, is victor (jayin)—and even in the south if powerful (baliri).
